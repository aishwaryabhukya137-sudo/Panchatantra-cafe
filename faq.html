A few helpful answers

Good to know.

Planning a visit? Here are a few quick answers to help you make the most of your time at Panchatantra Café.

Where is Panchatantra Café?

We're at House 1-10-290/1, Sai Nilayam, Circle 30, Begumpet, Hyderabad. Get directions ↗

What are your opening hours?

We're open every day from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Where can I see the menu?

Our live menu is available through Petpooja. Open the existing Petpooja menu ↗

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Panchatantra Café is pet-friendly and welcomes pets.

Can I read books at the café?

Yes. Books are available to everyone.

Are games available?

Yes. Chess and UNO are available to everyone.

Can I host a workshop or flea market?

Yes. Events and flea markets require 20 days' advance notice; workshops require 10 days. Please read the full event guidelines.

How can I contact the café?

Call or WhatsApp +91 85169 23456, or message us on Instagram at @panchatantrahyd.

How can I leave a review?

Visit our Google Maps listing and share your experience. Open Google Maps ↗
