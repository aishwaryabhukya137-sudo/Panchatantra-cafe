Before you enquire

  • Events and flea markets require at least 20 days' advance notice.
  • Workshops require at least 10 days' advance notice.
  • 50% advance payment is mandatory and non-refundable in case of cancellation.
  • Setup time must be included in booked hours.
  • Food and beverages are provided by Panchatantra Café unless otherwise agreed.
  • Organisers must follow all event guidelines and obtain required permissions.
