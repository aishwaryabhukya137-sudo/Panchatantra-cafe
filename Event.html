Events & Workshops

Panchatantra Café welcomes events, workshops, flea markets and creative gatherings. Please review the guidelines below before registering your event.

Event Guidelines & Policies

1. Advance Notice

  • Events and flea markets must be booked and informed at least 20 days in advance.
  • Workshops must be booked and informed at least 10 days in advance.
  • This ensures sufficient time for both parties to promote the event effectively.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • There is no cancellation policy.
  • Events, workshops and flea markets are promoted on social media.
  • 50% advance payment is mandatory and non-refundable in case of cancellation.

3. Setup and Dismantling

  • Setup time must be included within the booked hours.
  • The event organiser is responsible for ensuring the area is cleared and restored to its original condition after the event.

4. Property Usage

  • No damage to café property is allowed.
  • Any damages caused will be charged to the organiser.
  • Certain areas of the café may be restricted unless pre-approved.

5. Noise Levels

Noise must be kept to a reasonable level to avoid disturbing other patrons and neighbours.

6. Participant Limit

  • Events and workshops must adhere to the maximum allowed capacity of the café.
  • Additional attendees require prior approval.

7. Food and Beverages

All food and beverages must be provided by Panchatantra Café unless otherwise agreed upon.

8. Licenses and Permissions

The organiser is responsible for obtaining any necessary licenses, permits and permissions required for the event.

9. Electrical Equipment

  • Use of high-powered electrical equipment must be pre-approved.
  • Additional charges may apply for electricity usage.

10. Cleanliness

  • The organiser must ensure the space is left clean and tidy.
  • Additional cleaning charges may apply if the space is not restored to its original condition.

11. Brand Representation

Any representation of Panchatantra Café's brand in promotional materials must be pre-approved.
